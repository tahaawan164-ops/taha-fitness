How to use this app

  • Food & Water: tap what you ate or drank. Calories, protein and water are tracked for you.
  • Workout: shows today's exercises. Enter weight and reps, then save. The app suggests your next weight.
  • Body: enter your weight, steps, sleep and measurements every day or week.
  • Progress: charts, photos, a weekly report and the auto update history.
  • Deload: every 4th week and after nights with under 6 hours of sleep, the app lowers the workout volume so you recover.
  • Mode: 🌙 switches between dark and light mode.
If you have an illness or feel pain during a workout, see a doctor first.
